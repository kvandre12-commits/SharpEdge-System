Deep candlestick encyclopedia (26 entries)
Candlestick names describe auction events. Tradeability still requires location, participation/order flow, acceptance, and positive expectancy.
Doji
1-candle · single / indecision
Anatomy: Tiny real body; open and close nearly equal; wick mix can vary.
Auction read: Auction paused. Neither side owned the close.
Where it matters: Most useful after an extended push, at VWAP, ORH/ORL, PDH/PDL, gamma walls, or prior swing levels.
Confirm: Break and hold beyond the doji high/low with participation and acceptance.
Invalid: Chop back through the body or low-volume boundary poke.
False positive: In midday balance, tiny dojis are mostly noise confetti. Very decorative. Very useless.
Dragonfly doji
1-candle · single / tail rejection
Anatomy: Tiny body near the high with a long lower wick and little/no upper wick.
Auction read: Sellers pushed lower but buyers reclaimed the close.
Where it matters: Best at support, PDL/ORL/VWAP reclaim, LVN rejection, or downside exhaustion after a selloff.
Confirm: Next candle closes above the dragonfly high and retest holds above its midpoint/high.
Invalid: Lose the dragonfly low or fail immediately below VWAP/resistance.
False positive: A long lower wick during a strong downtrend can be absorption before continuation, not magic demand fairy dust.
Gravestone doji
1-candle · single / tail rejection
Anatomy: Tiny body near the low with a long upper wick and little/no lower wick.
Auction read: Buyers probed higher but sellers rejected the close.
Where it matters: Best at resistance, PDH/ORH/VWAP rejection, call wall, or upside exhaustion after a squeeze.
Confirm: Next candle closes below the gravestone low and failed retest holds under its midpoint/low.
Invalid: Reclaim the gravestone high with acceptance and volume.
False positive: In strong trend-up, this can be simple profit-taking before continuation.
Spinning top
1-candle · single / indecision
Anatomy: Small body with meaningful upper and lower wicks.
Auction read: Both sides probed; neither side finished the auction with control.
Where it matters: Useful as a pause marker after impulse or inside compression near a known reference.
Confirm: Expansion through either wick extreme with relative volume and acceptance.
Invalid: Repeated overlap around the body; no range expansion.
False positive: Calling every spinning top a reversal is how accounts learn slapstick.
Hammer / demand tail
1-candle · single / tail rejection
Anatomy: Small/medium body near the high; long lower wick, small upper wick.
Auction read: Lower prices were rejected; buyers defended into the close.
Where it matters: Matters after a push down into support, VWAP reclaim, put wall, ORL failed breakdown, or gap-fill area.
Confirm: Close above the hammer high; better if pullback holds above the hammer body.
Invalid: Break below the tail low or reclaim fails at nearby overhead supply.
False positive: A hammer in the middle of nowhere is just a candle wearing a cape.
Hanging man
1-candle · single / distribution warning
Anatomy: Hammer-like body after an up-push: long lower wick, small upper wick.
Auction read: Sellers finally forced a downside test; buyers recovered, but control is no longer clean.
Where it matters: Most meaningful after extended upside into resistance, PDH/ORH, call wall, or upper expected-move band.
Confirm: Bearish case confirms on a close below the hanging-man low with failed reclaim.
Invalid: Acceptance above the high with trend participation.
False positive: Without prior advance, it is not a hanging man. Context, butcher. Context.
Inverted hammer
1-candle · single / reversal probe
Anatomy: Small body near the low/mid with long upper wick after decline.
Auction read: Buyers tested supply above; first probe failed, but demand showed up.
Where it matters: Useful near support after downside momentum when the next candle can reclaim the wick.
Confirm: Next candle takes and holds the inverted-hammer high.
Invalid: Lose the low or fail under VWAP/resistance with weak volume.
False positive: The pattern is early; without follow-through it is just rejected upside.
Shooting star / supply tail
1-candle · single / tail rejection
Anatomy: Small body near the low with long upper wick after an up-push.
Auction read: Higher prices were rejected; sellers won the close.
Where it matters: Matters into resistance, VWAP rejection, ORH/PDH failed breakout, call wall, or exhausted squeeze.
Confirm: Close below the star low; stronger if retest rejects the body/midpoint.
Invalid: Reclaim and accept above the star high.
False positive: In a strong trend, stars can be pause candles before another leg up.
Bullish/Bearish marubozu
1-candle · single / conviction
Anatomy: Large real body with very small wicks.
Auction read: One side controlled nearly the whole interval.
Where it matters: Useful when it breaks a known level or initiates from VWAP/OR range with participation.
Confirm: Acceptance beyond the close and midpoint defense on retest.
Invalid: Immediate midpoint loss/reclaim against the candle direction.
False positive: Late marubozu after a stretched move can mark exhaustion, not continuation.
Strong bull/bear candle
1-candle · single / pressure
Anatomy: Body dominates more than half the range; wick balance is secondary.
Auction read: Close shows directional control for that bar.
Where it matters: Useful only with relative volume, level interaction, and follow-through context.
Confirm: Next bar holds the midpoint or expands in the same direction.
Invalid: Fast fade through midpoint or failure at known level.
False positive: One strong candle inside chop is a bullhorn in a closet.
Bullish/Bearish engulfing
2-candle · two-candle / reversal
Anatomy: Second body fully swallows prior opposite-color body.
Auction read: Control flips from one side to the other across two bars.
Where it matters: Best after a push into a major reference level, failed break, or exhaustion zone.
Confirm: Follow-through beyond the engulfing high/low plus retest acceptance.
Invalid: Price falls back inside the engulfed body and cannot reclaim direction.
False positive: Engulfing in balanced chop often just means range expansion without edge.
Piercing line
2-candle · two-candle / bullish reclaim
Anatomy: Green candle closes above midpoint of prior red body but below prior open.
Auction read: Sellers extended; buyers reclaimed more than half the prior auction.
Where it matters: Best at support or after a failed breakdown where reclaim has participation.
Confirm: Acceptance above prior open; hold above midpoint on pullback.
Invalid: Reject prior midpoint/open and lose current low.
False positive: Without downside context it is just partial recovery, not a setup.
Dark cloud cover
2-candle · two-candle / bearish rejection
Anatomy: Red candle closes below midpoint of prior green body but above prior open.
Auction read: Buyers extended; sellers reclaimed more than half the prior auction.
Where it matters: Best at resistance or after a failed breakout where rejection has participation.
Confirm: Acceptance below prior open; failed retest of midpoint/body.
Invalid: Reclaim prior midpoint/high with acceptance.
False positive: In trend-up this can be a shallow pause before continuation.
Harami / inside body
2-candle · two-candle / compression
Anatomy: Second body is contained inside prior body.
Auction read: Momentum pauses; the auction compresses inside the prior body.
Where it matters: Useful near boundaries when compression defines a clean trigger box.
Confirm: Expansion out of the mother candle with participation and acceptance.
Invalid: Boundary poke that immediately returns inside the mother candle.
False positive: Inside-body guessing is premium-donation behavior. Don’t be generous to market makers.
Inside bar
2-candle · two-candle / compression
Anatomy: Entire range sits inside the prior candle range.
Auction read: Volatility contracts; future expansion matters more than current direction.
Where it matters: Best around OR/VWAP/major levels where both breakout and trap routes are visible.
Confirm: Breakout/breakdown holds beyond mother-bar range with volume.
Invalid: Failed break back inside range; treat as trap/fade candidate only if spine agrees.
False positive: Inside bars are not bullish or bearish by themselves. They are loaded springs, not arrows.
Outside bar
2-candle · two-candle / expansion
Anatomy: Current high exceeds prior high and current low breaks prior low.
Auction read: Auction swept both sides; close location tells who won the expansion.
Where it matters: Useful at failed breaks, liquidity sweeps, news candles, or volatility expansion windows.
Confirm: Direction requires close beyond one side and acceptance on retest.
Invalid: Close back near center with no follow-through; both sides got chopped.
False positive: Outside bars during catalysts can be pure whipsaw. Respect event radar.
Tweezer top/bottom
2-candle · two-candle / repeated test
Anatomy: Two candles print nearly equal highs or lows.
Auction read: Auction retested the same level and failed to make fresh progress.
Where it matters: Best at known reference levels, range edges, gamma walls, or prior swing extremes.
Confirm: Move away from the shared level with close beyond both candle bodies.
Invalid: Break and accept through the shared high/low.
False positive: Equal highs/lows inside tight chop are normal noise, not sacred geometry.
Morning star
3-candle · three-candle / bullish reversal
Anatomy: Strong red candle, small pause candle, then green reclaim through first candle midpoint.
Auction read: Sell pressure exhausts, pauses, then buyers reclaim control.
Where it matters: Best after a selloff into support, ORL/PDL failed breakdown, VWAP reclaim, or put wall.
Confirm: Close above the third candle high; pullback holds above star/body midpoint.
Invalid: Lose the star low or reject below the first candle midpoint.
False positive: Without prior decline, it is just three candles looking dramatic.
Evening star
3-candle · three-candle / bearish reversal
Anatomy: Strong green candle, small pause candle, then red rejection through first candle midpoint.
Auction read: Buy pressure exhausts, pauses, then sellers reclaim control.
Where it matters: Best after an up-push into resistance, ORH/PDH failed breakout, VWAP rejection, or call wall.
Confirm: Close below the third candle low; failed retest holds under the star/body midpoint.
Invalid: Reclaim above the star high or first candle midpoint with acceptance.
False positive: Without prior advance, it is not an evening star. It’s just astrology with OHLC.
Three white soldiers
3-candle · three-candle / continuation
Anatomy: Three strong green candles with rising closes.
Auction read: Buyers walk price higher bar by bar.
Where it matters: Most useful after base breakout, VWAP reclaim, or trend day confirmation.
Confirm: Fourth/pullback bar holds above sequence midpoint and volume stays constructive.
Invalid: Fast fade of the third candle or loss of second candle midpoint.
False positive: Late sequence can mean exhaustion. Great soldiers sometimes march off cliffs.
Three black crows
3-candle · three-candle / continuation
Anatomy: Three strong red candles with falling closes.
Auction read: Sellers walk price lower bar by bar.
Where it matters: Most useful after support break, VWAP rejection, or trend-down confirmation.
Confirm: Bounce fails below sequence midpoint and sellers hold lower highs.
Invalid: Reclaim of second/third candle midpoint with participation.
False positive: Late crows after a big dump can be capitulation/exhaustion instead of fresh edge.
Three inside up/down
3-candle · three-candle / inside breakout
Anatomy: Large candle, inside/reclaim candle, then break beyond the first candle extreme.
Auction read: Compression inside the mother candle resolves through one boundary.
Where it matters: Best when the mother candle tags a meaningful level and breakout aligns with acceptance.
Confirm: Hold beyond the mother candle high/low with retest support/resistance.
Invalid: Return inside the mother candle; trap risk takes over.
False positive: A break without participation is just a boundary poke wearing a fake mustache.
Three outside up/down
3-candle · three-candle / engulfing continuation
Anatomy: Engulfing pair followed by third candle closing beyond the engulfing direction.
Auction read: Control flips, then proves follow-through on the next bar.
Where it matters: Best at major references after failed break/exhaustion, not in random mid-range chop.
Confirm: Third candle extreme holds on retest; midpoint of engulfing body remains defended.
Invalid: Third candle fully fades or price re-enters engulfed body.
False positive: If the third candle is climactic into a wall, continuation can turn into exhaustion.
Kicker
2-candle · two-candle / regime shock
Anatomy: Opposite-color candle opens/gaps away from prior body and drives strongly the other way.
Auction read: New information reprices the auction; prior side is trapped.
Where it matters: Most meaningful around news, event radar catalysts, open drive, or hard level rejection.
Confirm: No immediate gap fill; acceptance holds beyond the kicker body.
Invalid: Fast fill back into prior candle body.
False positive: On 1m data, micro gaps can be feed artifacts. Verify source and spread.
Rising/Falling three methods
5-candle · five-candle / continuation
Anatomy: Large impulse candle, three small countertrend candles contained inside it, then continuation candle.
Auction read: Trend pauses without surrendering the impulse range, then resumes.
Where it matters: Best in trend day context with pullback holding VWAP/impulse midpoint.
Confirm: Continuation candle closes beyond impulse extreme with participation.
Invalid: Countertrend candles break the impulse candle low/high or VWAP fails.
False positive: If the pause candles have heavy opposing volume, it may be distribution/absorption, not healthy rest.
Abandoned baby
3-candle · three-candle / gap reversal
Anatomy: Strong candle, isolated doji gap, then opposite strong candle leaving the doji abandoned.
Auction read: Auction overextended, paused in isolation, then repriced opposite.
Where it matters: More relevant on daily/session charts than noisy 1m SPY feed.
Confirm: Opposite candle holds and the gap/island is not reclaimed.
Invalid: Price fills back through the doji island.
False positive: Rare on intraday liquid ETFs; don’t hallucinate it from tiny micro-gaps.
